5 FACTSS ABOUT GEORGE RUSSEL

He is British Formula 1 Driver

George Russell was born on 15 February 1998 in King's Lynn, England. He developed an interest in racing at a young age and started karting when he was a child. Through years of training and competition, he worked his way up through different racing categories before reaching Formula 1

He Races for the Mercedes Team

George Russell drives for the Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS Formula One Team. He joined Mercedes as a full-time driver in 2022 after racing for Williams. Driving for a top team requires excellent driving skills, teamwork, and the ability to make quick decisions under pressure.

He Won His First Formula 1 Grand Prix in 2022

George Russell achieved his first Formula 1 Grand Prix victory at the São Paulo Grand Prix in Brazil in November 2022. He also won the sprint race at the same circuit earlier that weekend. This was a significant achievement because it showed that he could compete at the highest level and turn his hard work into success.

He Started His Formula 1 Career with Williams

George Russell made his Formula 1 debut in 2019 with the Williams Racing team. Although Williams was struggling to compete with the fastest teams at that time, he continued to show his potential and gained valuable experience. In 2020, he also temporarily drove for Mercedes at the Sakhir Grand Prix

His Racing Number Is 63

George Russell is well known for using the number 63 on his Formula 1 car. The number has a personal meaning because it is connected to his older brother, Benjy Russell, who used the number in karting. George decided to continue using it as he progressed through his racing career.
